The partners jointly operated sugar cane plantations in Barbados, frequenting West African ports to purchase enslaved persons, and Amsterdam and England to sell rum, molasses, and sugar. Shelter Island would serve as a strategic base for the North American branch of their business. With yet another new claim of European ownership to this land, The Manhansett were forced to further reckon with the complex political and economic realities throughout the 17th century and beyond."
