This essay collection brings together diverse perspectives on the Sylvester Manor Archival Collection, inviting readers to consider how the collection’s artifacts offer windows into the complexities of this site’s past. 

Historians, archivists, and public historians from New York University approach these materials by interrogating how archives are constructed, how historical value is assigned, and how meaning is produced and contested over time.

Archaeologists from the University of Massachusetts, Boston, contribute insights from their fieldwork at Sylvester Manor beginning in 1998. Their essays, drawing on previous research, contextualize the artifacts they recovered and explore the material traces of lived experience at the site.

We invite you to explore this digital exhibit as both an introduction to the collection and a space for critical reflection on the processes of archival preservation, interpretation, and storytelling.
